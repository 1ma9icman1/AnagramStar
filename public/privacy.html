Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 28, 2026

1. Overview

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Anagram Star ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you play our game via Discord Activity, web, or mobile.

2. Information We Collect

Anagram Star is designed with a privacy-first, minimal data footprint:

  • Local Gameplay Data: High scores, games played counter, and custom avatar/nickname preferences are stored locally in your browser/client via localStorage.
  • Discord Identity (when applicable): When played inside Discord, your public username and avatar are used strictly for in-game display and match scoring.
  • No Personal Tracking: We do not collect or sell personally identifiable information (PII), email addresses, or payment data.

3. How We Use Information

Any data processed is used exclusively for:

  • Rendering your game score, word count, and leaderboard results.
  • Allowing you to share challenge links with friends on Discord.
  • Maintaining game settings (sound preferences, timer lengths).

4. Third-Party Services

When using Anagram Star through Discord, your use is also subject to Discord's Privacy Policy. Hosted deployments (such as Vercel) may log standard technical web traffic for operational reliability.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your profile and high scores are saved locally on your client device, you can delete all data at any time by clearing your browser cache and local storage.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

7. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via Discord or the project repository.
